# Moonlight Zoo character notes

These details are story canon and should stay consistent across narration,
illustration, interactions, and audio direction.

## Gia

- The hero and source of the game’s choices.
- Smart, capable, funny, and never spoken down to.
- Repetition is noticed warmly and may become an important story detail.

## Penguin

- Gia’s best friend and beloved stuffed penguin, carried everywhere.
- Always enters an adventure with Gia and always appears in its retelling.
- Clearly a well-loved plush toy. Magic may let Penguin move, react, or gain a
  temporary superpower, but the game should not replace or lose Penguin.
- Current art is provisional until a photo of the real Penguin is available.

## Nova Kitty

- An original moonlight superhero cat and encouraging adventure guide.
- Energetic, confident, kind, and calm when giving instructions.

## Luma

- Male moon jellyfish; he/him.
- Warm, musical, bioluminescent, and delighted by repeated bell playing.
- His coral bow is a bow tie.

## Octopus

- Male; he/him.
- Personality, name, appearance, and specific superpower are still open.
